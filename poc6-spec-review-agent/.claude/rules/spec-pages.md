# Spec pages

Rule tag: P6-RULE-SPEC

- The pages are test data with planted problems. Never correct, reword or reformat them: the scorer and the quote
  check depend on the exact text.
- Item IDs (`SC-01-F01`, `CMN-R02`, `MSG-E001`) are the only way to cite a place. Quote a cell or a sentence exactly as
  written, without adding 「。」 or joining two passages.
